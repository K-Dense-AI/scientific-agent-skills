## Apply the relevant question

- NCA/BE: identify formulation, fasting/fed state, metric, sampling and jurisdiction; M13A is not a
  substitute for every special-design or product-specific requirement.
- Population PK/ER: predefine the decision, data provenance, model assumptions, uncertainty and
  evaluation relevant to that use. Synthetic fit recovery is software evidence, not clinical evidence.
- DDI: distinguish screening trigger from AUCR prediction and clinical action. Input uncertainty,
  metabolites and pathways outside the simplified model remain material.
- Pediatrics/FIH: integrate biology, exposure-response, species/population relevance and safety;
  a numerical exposure match or conservative calculator output alone is insufficient.
